# CSCS Job Hunter

Downloads for the CSCS Job Hunter desktop app (Manhattan Associates, Blue Yonder and AI job postings in one place).

## Install (first time)

Open the [latest release](https://github.com/arunr-cscs/cscs-job-hunter/releases/latest) and download:

- **Windows:** `CSCS-Job-Hunter.zip`. Right-click it > Extract All, then double-click **Install CSCS Job Hunter.bat** in the extracted folder.
- **Mac:** `CSCS-Job-Hunter-Mac.zip`. Double-click it, then double-click **Install CSCS Job Hunter.command** (the first time: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway). It takes 5-10 minutes.

No admin rights are needed. Your jobs, marks, resumes and settings stay on your computer.

## Updates

You don't need to download again. When a new version is out, the app shows a bar at the top: click **Update now**.
The version number next to "Job Hunter" in the app checks for updates on demand.
